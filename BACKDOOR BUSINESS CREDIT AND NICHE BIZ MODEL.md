## **4\. UCC-1 Inter-Entity Lending: Does It Still Work?**

Short answer:

* Filing a UCC-1 to show a legitimate inter-entity loan does establish a public record, and it can support your business’s “seasoned” credit file, especially if you also show actual banking activity (i.e., you really move funds from entity to entity).

* But: Bureaus and lenders have gotten wise.

  * A UCC-1 alone doesn’t move the needle unless there’s a real note, real loan, and evidence of funds movement.

  * The best use case: Pair the UCC-1 with an actual loan (bank statement, executed note, and regular payments between your entities). Now you can reference “secured installment loan” on your file.

  * Some underwriters will ask for proof of payment/receipts between entities, especially for big purchases (like $150k car).

So, in 2025: UCC-1 is best as part of a stack, not a solo show pony.

It’s worth doing as a backup, but it won’t be the single “yes” for modern AI-driven underwriters unless the paper trail is thick.
